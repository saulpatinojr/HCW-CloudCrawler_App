# Code of conduct

## Expectations

Everyone taking part in this repository, in issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and commits, is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent;
- give and accept review on the work, not the person;
- keep discussion on the problem at hand and the evidence for it;
- respect decisions recorded in the repository and raise disagreement through
  a new issue or decision record rather than by ignoring them.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers named in `.github/CODEOWNERS`
through the private channel described in `.github/SECURITY.md`. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that
violates this document and may restrict the participation of those who do.
